## Prior and upstream work

This project builds interoperability tooling around work provided by:

- Goodix, author of the proprietary fingerprint module;
- Lenovo, distributor of the official Linux TOD package;
- Canonical and Ubuntu libfprint maintainers, providers of TOD-enabled
  libfprint packaging;
- freedesktop.org libfprint and fprintd contributors;
- community researchers and packagers documenting Goodix Linux support.

Contributors retain copyright in their respective work. Inclusion here does
not change the licenses of upstream projects or proprietary artifacts.
